# Future Platform Roadmap

This document outlines the mid-term goals, long-term roadmap, and conceptual ideas for the PetalPath platform.

---

## 1. Planned (Next Major Phases)

*   **Active Spaced Repetition**: Activation of spacing intervals and reinforcement queues to automate remediation scheduling.
*   **Star Accumulation Rules Engine**: Rules-based evaluation checking star thresholds to trigger sticker rewards and badges.
*   **Daily Streaks Logging**: Event triggers tracking multi-day consistencies.

---

## 2. Backlog / Ideas

*   **Offline Cache Synchronization**: Enabling client databases to queue completions offline and sync securely upon reconnect.
*   **Enhanced Sound & Modalities Integration**: Adding creative modalities (e.g. tactile drawing gestures controls, sound matching guides).

---

## 3. Vision (3 - 5 Years)

*   **Pedagogical Guidance Assistants**: Expanding virtual mentors to provide speech guidance, contextual prompts, and encouragement metrics customized via child performance records.
*   **Advanced Personalization**: The recommendation engine may evolve beyond rules-based thresholds using data-driven classification algorithms once sufficient learning logs exist across profiles.
*   **Classrooms and School Ecosystems**: Supporting teacher dashboards, school roster synchronizations, and unified classroom analytics models.
*   **Curriculum Curator Interfaces**: Providing administrative tools for content managers to edit skills graphs and prerequisites locks without touching code.
